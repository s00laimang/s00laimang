One line, then the work

Your 5-word post ("loving this services card 😍") beat the long one. Showcase posts that do well on X are usually one sentence that leads with the thing and lets the image or video speak. Write lowercase, like texting a friend. Extra context goes in a reply to yourself.

Concept sites as client work

Brisbane Party Hire, Dream Clean and Virzi are concepts. Name them and tell the story, but never call them clients. The Dream Clean "founder" photo is AI-generated, so never post it as a real person.

Pitch details

No owners' names, emails or handles. Don't tag the businesses, and don't post the problems you found on their current sites (that's for the pitch). Let each owner hear from you before they see it on X.

Private details

Names, phone numbers, ABNs, the lead tracker, business revenue or transaction numbers, API keys and private dashboard URLs.
